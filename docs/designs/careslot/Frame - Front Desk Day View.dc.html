DES-009 · Front Desk Day View

Epic E004 · serves US-007, FR-DESK-001, AC-021, AC-022

  • Layout (designer’s choice): one Card per doctor (max 420 px each) side by side; the row scrolls sideways if there are more doctors than fit.
  • Every 20-minute slot of each doctor’s working hours is listed: booked ones show patient and reason with Open (→ DES-011); free ones show Book (→ DES-010).
  • Changing the date reloads the cards (htmx, Loading… meanwhile).
  • Breakpoints: desktop only (staff work on desktops, PRD personas).
  • Session timeout: after 30 minutes without activity the next click opens Sign in with the idle message (DES-002).
  • Names, dates and clinics in this frame are sample content.
