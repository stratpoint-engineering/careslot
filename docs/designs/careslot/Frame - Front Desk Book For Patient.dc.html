DES-010 · Front Desk Book For Patient

Epic E004 · serves US-008, FR-DESK-002, AC-023, AC-024, AC-025, AC-026

  • Opened from a free slot’s Book link (DES-009), so the slot is filled in. Walk-ins are booked the same way.
  • Search runs as you type (htmx). Selecting a patient fills the Appointment block.
  • No override of the 2-appointment limit for staff (AC-026).
  • After Book: back to the day view; the slot shows the patient.
  • Breakpoints: desktop only (staff work on desktops, PRD personas).
  • Session timeout: after 30 minutes without activity the next click opens Sign in with the idle message (DES-002).
  • Names, dates and clinics in this frame are sample content.
